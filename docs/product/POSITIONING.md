# MeetLens Product Positioning

## 1. Product Overview

MeetLens is a mobile-first AI meeting assistant that captures live audio from in-person or online meetings, generates real-time transcripts, translates them on the fly, and produces structured summaries and action items when the meeting ends.

Instead of being a bot tied to Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams, MeetLens works anywhere you can hear the meeting: you simply place your phone on the table (or near your laptop speakers), and MeetLens listens, transcribes, translates, and summarizes.

---

## 2. Problem

### Core Problems for Knowledge Workers, Freelancers, and Consultants

- Multilingual meetings are hard to follow if you’re not native in the main language.
- Taking notes while actively participating in a meeting is cognitively expensive.
- Many people leave meetings unsure about key decisions and next steps.
- Existing solutions are often:
    - Tied to a specific platform (only Zoom/only Google Meet/only Teams)
    - Desktop-first, not mobile-first
    - Focused on either transcription OR translation OR summary, not all three seamlessly combined
    - Complex to set up (invite bots, connect calendars, manage integrations)

---

## 3. Solution

MeetLens acts as a “lens” over your meetings:

- **Real-time transcription:** Converts spoken language into text as the meeting happens.
- **Real-time translation:** Shows a live translation stream for non-native speakers.
- **Post-meeting summary:** At the end of the meeting, MeetLens generates a structured summary with:
    - Short overview
    - Action items
    - Decisions

All of this runs from a simple mobile app – no bots, no complex setup, no platform lock-in.

---

## 4. Target Users & Use Cases

### Primary Target Segment

- **Freelancers, consultants, coaches, and solo professionals** who:
    - Join many client calls in different tools (Zoom, Meet, Teams, phone calls)
    - Work with international clients (multilingual context)
    - Need clean notes and action items to deliver follow-ups and reports

### Secondary Segments

- Students attending lectures or seminars in a non-native language
- Remote workers in international teams
- Small agencies and boutique firms without enterprise IT setups

### Key Use Cases

- Client discovery calls
- Project status meetings
- Coaching/therapy sessions
- Sales calls with foreign clients
- Internal cross-language alignment meetings

---

## 5. Differentiation vs. Existing Solutions

### Not Just Another “Meeting Bot”

Most AI meeting tools today:

- Require inviting a bot to Zoom/Meet/Teams.
- Are primarily desktop/web experiences.
- Focus on transcription + summary but **not** real-time translation.

MeetLens is different:

- **Mobile-first:** Works wherever your phone can hear the meeting (in-person, laptop speakers, even phone calls on speaker).
- **Platform-independent:** Not tied to a single conferencing tool.
- **Real-time translation + transcription:** Designed from day one to support multilingual meetings.

### Against Transcription-Only Tools

- Tools like Otter/Fireflies focus heavily on transcription and summaries.
- MeetLens adds a strong **real-time translation** layer for non-native speakers.

### Against Translation-Only Tools

- Generic voice translators (e.g., travel translator apps) handle short phrases, not 60–90-minute meetings.
- They don’t provide:
    - Continuous transcripts
    - Meeting notes
    - Action item extraction

MeetLens combines transcription, translation, and structured meeting notes in a single, streamlined flow.

## 6. Core Value Proposition

**One sentence:**

> MeetLens helps you truly understand your meetings in any language and captures all the important details for you – live transcripts, live translation, and clear action items from your phone.
> 

### Value Pillars

1. **Clarity in any language**
    - Real-time subtitles + translation for multilingual meetings.
2. **Cognitive offload**
    - No more splitting attention between listening and note-taking.
3. **Reliable follow-up**
    - Structured summaries and action items reduce forgotten tasks and misalignment.
4. **Anywhere, with anyone**
    - Works in-person, on Zoom, on Meet, on Teams, or on a phone call – as long as your phone can hear it.

## 7. MVP Scope (Positioning for v1)

For the MVP, MeetLens will focus on:

- Single-user mobile app (no teams, no shared workspaces yet)
- One main language pair (e.g., source: English, target: Turkish or German) with room to extend
- Real-time transcription and translation view
- End-of-meeting summary with:
    - Short overview
    - Action items
    - Decisions

Deliberately **out of scope** for v1:

- Pricing plans, subscriptions, and usage limits
- Team collaboration features
- Calendar integration and auto-join
- Multi-speaker diarization
- On-device offline models

The goal of v1 is to prove:

- Technical feasibility (latency, stability, accuracy)
- User delight ("wow, this actually helps me understand meetings")
- Clear before/after value in real user scenarios.

## 9. Future Direction (Beyond MVP)

In future iterations, MeetLens can evolve towards:

- Multi-language support with automatic language detection
- Team workspaces and shared meeting libraries
- Calendar integration and 1-tap meeting start from events
- Topic-based summaries and CRM/task manager integrations (Notion, ClickUp, Trello, etc.)
- Advanced personalization (tone of summary, level of detail, role-based views)

But the core positioning remains the same:

**MeetLens is the simplest way to make any meeting understandable, memorable, and actionable – right from your phone.**
